# E3 verification: native worker construction

Branch: `economy-e3`, based on E1 `1a17708`. Implementation commits `4a48ab4`, `b7e6f4b`, `d1e3a03`, `e635618`, `6bd3c0e`, `fa43763` and `eda238d`. E2 gathering controls are also present as a dependency and are measured separately in `docs/player-economy-e2-verification.md`. All licensed captures, map products, runtime logs, browser profiles and build products are ignored under USB-backed `build/` and `run-data/`.

## Implementation and authority

Finite-mode construction reserves a park site and journals a `build:<player>:<request>` intent before one BWAPI SCV build order. Native acceptance is shown separately from creation. Both OpenBW participants watch the same type, owner and upper-left tile, then Go binds their exact native IDs before OpenRCT2's projected scenery enters generic reconciliation. The projected native structure adds no duplicate navigation footprint. Its ordered park revision waits for both participants to apply the same hash at the scheduled frame. Native BWAPI controls cost, prerequisites, progress, completion, interruption, cancellation/refund and supply. The journal and projection link survive same-session bridge restart; repeated requests cannot replay an ambiguous order. A paired absence for 2,400 native frames publishes `native_creation_unconfirmed` while retaining the reservation and never issuing another build or refund.

Server-side finite-mode validation rejects unbound Terran scenery, direct free spawns/reinforcements, reserved-site park edits and manual removal/reconstruction of authored native assets. The old authored scenario behavior remains available outside finite mode. The seven supported Terran building options route through SCV construction; no Go or JavaScript economy simulation was added.

## Failures and limits preserved

The first all-building attempt on Frontier (`run-data/player-economy-e3-1793926418/`) was not a seven-building success. Barracks at 24,6 completed; Academy at 29,6 was accepted natively but no building was observed; Factory, Armory and Depot chosen there were unbuildable or unexplored. The targeted native scan in `run-data/player-economy-e3-1793982881/` reported `can_build=0` on those latter sites, even though the park geometry preview allowed the requests. Native BWAPI remains authoritative. The open flat fixture is a bounded authored test site for every type; it is not automatic ordinary-park placement. Its initial CHK Command Center lacks an authored park `scenario:base` target, so it cannot prove base production UI. Frontier has that target and proved native SCV training.

The E0/E1 full-snapshot initial/reconnect navigation mask offset of two frames remains visible. E3 ordered live revisions 2–8 and combat revision 3 used the confirmed same-hash/same-frame protocol; they do not assert that the earlier full-snapshot offset vanished. Unsupported wall proxies near the Frontier base remain at the E1 count; no global collision or exploration bypass was made.

The bridge-restart run bound the same native ID from publisher/controller observations at frames 2208/2196, then completed from paired frame 2616. Observation frames can differ during reconnect; the ordered revision mask still applied at the single confirmed frame 2342. This distinction prevents a restart observation from being mistaken for a confirmed park revision.

## E3 exit assessment

| Criterion | Assessment |
| --- | --- |
| Finite native funds govern buildings and units | Met on authored fixtures: all seven building costs and prerequisites, cancellation refund, Depot supply and subsequent SCV training were observed natively. |
| No ordinary player action creates free military assets | Met in the supported finite-mode routes by server-side patch, snapshot, direct-spawn and native-worker gates plus native low-funds rejection. A separate hostile network-peer live probe remains for E5. |
| Restart/retry cannot duplicate construction or debit | Met by the durable-journal regression, unconfirmed-creation live probe and in-flight bridge restart with one Depot/one debit. |

This assessment applies to E3 construction in fixed authored fixtures. It does not assert E4 ordinary-park economy placement or E5 release acceptance.

Browser evidence uses direct join to an isolated authored scenario. A normal stock New Game economy, waves, ordinary-map placement and deployment remain E4/E5. The browser probe submits actual player actions from the graphical WASM client; no literal human pointer sequence has been certified.

The 2,400-frame unconfirmed policy is deliberately conservative: it exposes a failed creation and persists the site reservation, since an accepted but ambiguous native order cannot safely be replayed or freed on absence alone. There is no automatic player-side recovery of that blocked site in this stage. Worker combat death during an incomplete build, native combat destruction of a completed newly projected building, a forged network peer, and concurrent stock scenery copy were not separately exercised live. Offline ledger/park-action regressions cover duplicate intents, stale geyser generation, overlapping reservations, free Terran scenery, reserved-site edits, authored base spoof/removal and direct spawn gating. These remaining live fault cases belong to the E5 integration matrix before release.
