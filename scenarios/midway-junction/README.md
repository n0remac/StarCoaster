# Midway Junction

Defend the Midway Ferris Wheel from east, north, and south Zerg attacks. Six Marines begin in the western staging garden; eight Zerglings begin at the eastern entrance. Command the squad through the existing OpenRCT2 controls and use **Spawn Marine** to reinforce at a clear park tile.

The western divider has a four-segment destructible brick gate. Before demolition, movement from the Marine staging area to the wheel must go around the north or south end. Demolishing the gate creates a shorter crossing for both sides. The north midway and south service route have separate obstacle arrangements.

Generate and play with `scripts/project.sh park midway-junction` and `scripts/project.sh play midway-junction`. The layout source is `park-layout.json`; generated park, fixture, and navigation exports are ignored under `run-data/midway-junction/`.
