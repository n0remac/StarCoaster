# Frontier Outpost

The playable 64×64 ridge park is generated by `scripts/create-park-assault.py --playable`.
`scenario.json` describes starting actors, the carousel objective, and the defense waves;
`mapping.json` preserves the destructible gate rule. The opening five arrive
on readiness, two approach from the south after 300 native frames,
and three arrive from the northeast after the southern wave is cleared.
Generated parks and native maps stay
under ignored `run-data/`.

Run `scripts/project.sh start` for full bootstrap, or
`scripts/project.sh play frontier-outpost` after building the engines.
Use `scripts/project.sh park frontier-outpost` to regenerate the park and
`scripts/project.sh record --scenario frontier-outpost --duration 85` to capture it.

The OpenBW carousel target currently has 40 effective hit points. A mapping
health override was tested during balancing but did not change the native
proxy's reported health, so this scenario keeps the existing target behavior.
The live victory probe orders and manually reinforces Marines through the
normal player actions; it does not add automatic Marines to gameplay.
